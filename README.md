## MagHeal
### Wearable Pulsed Electromagnetic Field Prototype

**[View MagHeal →](./MagHeal.pdf)**

MagHeal is a collaborative engineering project exploring a wearable pulsed electromagnetic field (PEMF) device. The prototype combines programmable pulse generation, magnetic-field sensing, and an adjustable sleeve designed to accommodate the electronics.

### Technical Work

- **Embedded control:** Arduino UNO firmware regulates pulse frequency, with a digital display providing real-time frequency feedback
- **Magnetic-field sensing:** A Hall-effect sensor detects active magnetic fields generated by the coil
- **Circuit verification:** Tinkercad simulation supports development and verification of the circuit
- **Mechanical design:** A dual-layer sleeve modeled in Fusion 360 and fabricated through 3D printing provides adjustable fit and separates electronics from direct skin contact

## HERA Research
### Mapping the Regener-Pfotzer Maximum

**[View HERA Research →](./HERA-Research.pdf)**

HERA investigates how cosmic-ray particle counts vary with altitude and detector orientation through coordinated high-altitude balloon flights. The research combines particle detectors, environmental measurements, telemetry, and post-flight analysis to study the altitude of maximum secondary-particle intensity.

### Research Methods

- **Instrumentation:** CosmicWatch and mDetect particle detectors, alongside Geiger counters, support measurements during balloon flights
- **Telemetry:** UTC-stamped position, pressure, temperature, magnetic-field measurements, and particle-count summaries provide flight context
- **Data processing:** Flight CSV logs are aligned by timestamp and aggregated into one-minute count-rate bins for comparison across altitude and launch locations
- **Detector comparison:** Stabilized and unstabilized measurements are compared to investigate orientation-related differences in recorded particle flux
- **Visualization:** Python Folium and Leaflet support interactive mapping of participating launch sites
